Current Research

Proteostasis in Eukaryotes

01 / FOCUS

Investigating mitochondrial-associated degradation (MAD) mechanisms governing clearance of cytosolic misfolded proteins in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

02 / KEY FINDING

Discovered increased ubiquitinated protein accumulation on the mitochondrial outer membrane during global misfolding stress conditions.

03 / PROTEIN TARGET

Elucidating the role of TOM70 in sensing and eliminating global misfolding stress at the mitochondrial surface.

04 / METHODOLOGY

Generated and screened a random mutant library of a model cytosolic protein to capture degradation-prone misfolded intermediates.
